Two mark-level columns, both driven by each college's own historical draw rate (density) — its typical share of any given stretch of the merit list, from the real Phase 1 pattern. "Likely to gather here" is the expected number, out of everyone tied at your exact mark, who'd end up associated with that specific college overall if the same pattern holds — this reflects the real scatter we found (the same mark shows up at many colleges at once, not just one). "Can get a Round 2 seat" narrows that down to how many of that gathered group can actually get a new seat there once Phase 2 seats fill, capped by the real seats that extend into your mark's range — it can never exceed "Likely to gather here". Both are "~" expected values, not guaranteed headcounts.

"Non-participating locals (>350) re-entering" models the 338 real local (OU) candidates on the 2026 Final Merit List, scoring above 350, who never received ANY seat in real MQ Phase 1 — unlike the "Not Reported" candidates (already removed from the live pool entirely), these candidates simply never participated, so whether they show up to compete in Phase 2/3 is genuinely uncertain. Scores of 350 and below are excluded, since at that depth the merit list is unlikely to matter for these 23 colleges' Phase 2/3 windows. The default is 100% (conservative) — assuming all 338 fully re-enter and compete for the very same Phase 2/3 seats; at 0% none of them return, matching the earlier version of this tool. The slider works by estimating, per college and using that college's own real Phase 1 draw density, how many of the re-entering candidates whose real score falls in that college's Phase 2 (or Phase 3) extension window would land there — that many of the extension seats are then treated as consumed by returners rather than being real extra room for you, tightening the P2/P3 cutoff accordingly. It never affects the real Phase 1 column.

What changed in this version

This version anchors Phase 1 directly on real 2026 outcomes: for each college, the closing score/rank shown is computed from the actual candidates who reported (joined) in the real MQ Phase 1 college-wise allotment, after removing candidates who were allotted but did not report (per the official "Not Reported" list). Removing non-reporters also changes the live local competition pool itself — your own S.No's local standing is recalculated against this smaller, live pool of 3,465 candidates (down from 3,778), not the full published local list. Phase 2 is then projected forward using the real, official Phase-2 seat matrix vacancy count per college (already net of non-reporters, confirmed), using each college's own real Phase 1 draw rate throughout. This version factors in the real, confirmed CQ Phase 3 seat increase at SVS and Mahavir, and a slider for real local candidates who never participated in Phase 1 but could re-enter later rounds — see the notes below. The earlier "Projection method" and "College draw pattern" toggles, and the "common candidates moving to CQ/MCC" and "Cat B→Cat A cascade" sliders, have all been removed — this version now always uses the real 2026 P1-anchored method with each college's own draw pattern, both previously the recommended defaults.

How the mechanisms work

Real Phase 1 closing (always real, never projected)

  • Each college's Phase 1 closing score/S.No comes from real, reported (not "not-reported") CTB-LOC-OPEN-GEN-P1 candidates only.
  • Example: Mamata Medical College, Khammam closed real Phase 1 at score 380 — much lower than the historical 2025-based projection suggested, exactly matching what was checked directly against the official allotment list. Kamineni Narketpally closed even lower, at 376.

Phase 2 projection — "Real 2026 P1-anchored" method (recommended)

  • Starting point: the real Phase 1 closing position at each college.
  • Base extension: the official Phase-2 seat matrix vacancy count for that college (real, published, already net of non-reporting).
  • Non-participating locals (>350) re-entering slider: of the local (OU) candidates on the 2026 Final Merit List who never appear anywhere in the real MQ Phase 1 college-wise allotment (not allotted, not not-reported, simply absent), 338 score above 350 — that subset is what this slider models, since below 350 is well past where these 23 colleges' Phase 2/3 windows are likely to reach. Since these candidates hold no Phase 1 seat, they aren't part of today's 3,465-candidate live pool, and it's genuinely unclear how many will show up to compete in Phase 2/3 (as opposed to candidates who WERE allotted a seat and chose not to report, who the official process already tracks as returning-eligible and who are excluded from the live pool for a different reason). This slider estimates, for each college's raw Phase 2 window (before this effect) and raw Phase 3 window, how many of the (percentage-scaled) 338 candidates have a real score falling inside that window, weights that count by the college's own draw density, exactly as for every other seat-conversion in this tool — and treats that many of the extension seats as consumed by returners rather than as real room for the live pool, shrinking the S.No extension for that phase accordingly, floored at zero. The default is 100% (all 338 assumed to return, the conservative reading); at 0% this has no effect at all.
  • Extension seats (base seat-matrix vacancy, net of the returning-candidates adjustment) are converted into an equivalent extension in local S.No units using each college's own real Phase-1 density (candidates per S.No unit near its closing point), then mapped to a real 2026 score using the exact live local merit list (no percentile estimation needed here — every position's real score is known).
  • Phase 3 (2026) is not yet real, so it is projected by applying the same Phase-1-to-Phase-2 extension a second time, as a simple continuation (plus the real SVS/Mahavir Cat B growth below, where it applies) — flagged separately since it is the one genuinely projected number in this method.

Real CQ Phase 3 seat increase — SVS and Mahavir

  • A confirmed +25 Cat A (TG Convener/CQ) seat increase for Phase 3 at each of these 2 colleges was applied using the reservation split you shared: at a private college, TG Cat A is 50% of total intake and TG Cat B (local management) is 29.75%.
  • Working backward, +25 Cat A seats implies the total intake at each college grew by 25 ÷ 0.50 = 50 seats. Applying the same 29.75% share to that total gives an implied +14.9 new local Cat B seats per college. This part is a derived estimate, not an official Cat B announcement — flag it as such if you get a direct figure.
  • This implied Cat B growth (+14.9 seats) is applied directly and unconditionally to that college's Phase 3 capacity, since it represents genuine new capacity, not a behavioral assumption.
  • Net effect: these 2 colleges get a real Phase 3 boost on top of the usual base churn continuation — the real +14.9 Cat B seats — then the same returning-candidates adjustment as every other college.

Feasibility rating

  • Your S.No is converted to your live local standing (out of 3,465, after removing non-reporters) and compared against each college's projected S.No thresholds.
  • Very Safe — clears P1 (real). Safe — clears P2. Borderline — only P3 would take you. Very Risky — below P3.

Other notes

  • Excludes: Deccan, Shadan, Ayaan (minority-only), Dr VRK Women's, Raja Rajeshwari (women-only), all dental (BDS) colleges.
  • Non-local (NL) candidates are excluded throughout — not real competition for local seats.
  • If your own S.No is on the official Not Reported list, this tool doesn't specially flag that — it only removes OTHER not-reported candidates from the competitive pool ahead of you.
